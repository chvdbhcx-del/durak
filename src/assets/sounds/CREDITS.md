# Sound credits

The card sounds in this folder are real recordings from **Casino Audio 1.1** by Kenney Vleugels
([kenney.nl](https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio)), released under
[Creative Commons Zero (CC0)](http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): free for personal
and commercial use, credit not required but given here. The full licence text is in
`LICENSE-kenney-casino-audio.txt`.

Files used (converted from OGG to mono MP3 so every browser and WebView plays them):
`card-slide-1..6` (cards dealt from the deck), `card-place-1..4` (a card laid on the table),
`card-shove-1..4` (the table swept into the discard or into a hand).
